## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist practising on my acoustic piano, **I want to** tap "Mikrofon starten" on a test page and see live which note, octave and cent deviation the app hears from my piano, together with level, clarity and diagnostics, **so that** I know early whether microphone listening on my iPad works well enough to build the rest of the app on it.

<objective>
Slice 3 of 6: a held note stays steady instead of flickering, and the user sees how loud the sound is, how sure the detection is, and where the pitch sits on a tuner bar.

Purpose: MIC-05 (held tone stable ≥ 2 s) and MIC-03 (level + clarity). These are exactly the values the piano checklist reads off, and the numbers Phase 3 calibration will need.
Output: PitchSmoother (TDD with a simulated 2.4 s decaying A4), CentAnzeige, PegelBalken ×2 inside the Messkarte.
</objective>
