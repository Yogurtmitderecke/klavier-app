## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist practising on my acoustic piano, **I want to** tap "Mikrofon starten" on a test page and see live which note, octave and cent deviation the app hears from my piano, together with level, clarity and diagnostics, **so that** I know early whether microphone listening on my iPad works well enough to build the rest of the app on it.

<objective>
Slice 5 of 6: the user sees a "Diagnose" panel next to the live values that shows what the iPad actually gave the app — sample rates, whether voice filters are off, audio state, mic name, launch mode, analysis rate, last event and raw error details.

Purpose: MIC-04. There is no Mac for remote debugging; a single landscape screenshot must be enough to diagnose iPad problems during the piano test and the D-07 improvement loop.
Output: diagnose() snapshot in the capture source, controller metrics, pure row builder (TDD), DiagnosePanel, two-column layout.
</objective>
