## Phase Goal

**As a** pianist practising on my acoustic piano, **I want to** tap "Mikrofon starten" on a test page and see live which note, octave and cent deviation the app hears from my piano, together with level, clarity and diagnostics, **so that** I know early whether microphone listening on my iPad works well enough to build the rest of the app on it.

<objective>
Slice 4 of 6: when the mic cannot start (permission denied, no mic, mic busy, no HTTPS, unknown) or delivers nothing, the user sees a clear German explanation with steps and can retry with one tap; going to the background stops the mic.

Purpose: MIC-01 must work in iPad Safari, and the user is a non-programmer without a Mac — every failure must explain itself on screen. Includes the iOS 26.5.2 NotAllowedError bug workaround.
Output: micErrors + startart (TDD), MicFehler, MicHinweis, insecure path with WarningBar, background/pagehide stop.
</objective>
